1. The Service

AgentHeaven provides a managed artificial-intelligence digital-employee service ("the Service") that, on your behalf:

  • Configures a digital employee to perform a defined role (e.g., research, inbox triage, customer support, repetitive task automation, internal knowledge assistance, ops/back-office tasks);

The Service is configured by us following an onboarding consultation with you. You provide us with access to the tools the agent needs (e.g., CRM, email, helpdesk, document store) and a description of the work to be done. We process source documents and connected-tool content during agent sessions, but do not store your source documents by default. We do store session and activity logs, and we store the customer-specific memory/wiki needed for your agent to improve over time. We manage the digital employee on an ongoing basis.
