1. The Service

AgentHeaven provides a managed artificial-intelligence receptionist and digital-employee service ("the Service") that, on your behalf:

  • Configures an AI receptionist to answer calls, identify itself as AI, ask the questions you configure, take messages and callback details, and send you a summary by SMS (the AI receptionist does not book appointments or transfer calls);
  • Configures a digital employee to perform another defined role (e.g., research, inbox triage, customer support, repetitive task automation, internal knowledge assistance, or ops/back-office tasks);
  • Hosts the agent on infrastructure operated by us and our hosting providers, using a model-agnostic routing layer over selected AI model providers;
  • Includes underlying compute, LLM, and agreed voice/telephony usage as part of the flat monthly subscription, subject to the configured scope and fair-use terms in Section 7;
  • Maintains a customer-specific long-term memory/wiki for the agent, containing relevant business information, workflows, preferences, and approved learning from sessions;
  • Continuously tunes the agent based on your feedback, session logs, the customer-specific memory/wiki, and our team's ongoing review, within the tuning-hours allowance of your subscription tier.

The Service is configured by us following an onboarding consultation with you. You provide us with access to the tools the agent needs (e.g., phone routing, calendar, CRM, email, helpdesk, or document store) and a description of the work to be done. For an AI receptionist, the configured runbook also records the greeting, AI disclosure, permitted topics, the information to collect from callers, and the number that receives the summary SMS. We process source documents, connected-tool content, and call content during agent sessions, but do not store source documents or raw call audio by default. We do store session and activity logs, which may include call transcripts, messages, summaries, and metadata, and we store the customer-specific memory/wiki needed for your agent to improve over time.
