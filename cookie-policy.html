Cookie Policy

Effective date: 14 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 14 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AgentHeaven uses cookies, browser storage, analytics, and advertising technologies on agentheaven.ai.

Current implementation note: The website stores the selected language in browser local storage. Meta Pixel and Google Analytics are configured through the cookie banner and only load after the relevant consent is saved.

1. Essential Storage

We use essential browser storage for basic site functionality, such as remembering your selected language and storing your cookie-consent choice. This is not used for advertising and is not shared with third parties.

2. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic, page performance, and conversion paths. Google Analytics may process device data, browser data, approximate location, page views, events, and identifiers depending on configuration. Analytics is loaded only after you give consent through the cookie banner, and runs with IP anonymisation enabled.

3. Advertising and Conversion Tracking

We may use Meta Pixel to measure advertising performance and lead conversions. Meta Pixel may process browser/device data, page views, lead events, advertising identifiers, and related metadata. Advertising tracking is loaded only after you give consent through the cookie banner. We do not use Meta Pixel for sensitive categories, detailed behavioural profiling, or cross-context tracking beyond basic campaign attribution.

4. Cookie and Storage Inventory

The table below lists the cookies and browser-storage entries the website may set. Items in the "Analytics" and "Advertising" rows are loaded only after your consent. Specific cookie names from Google Analytics and Meta Pixel are set by those providers; the names below reflect the providers' standard implementations and may vary as those services evolve.

Name / pattern Category Provider Purpose Duration
ah_lang Essential AgentHeaven (local storage) Remembers your selected interface language (EN / HU / DE). Until cleared by you
ah_cookie_consent Essential AgentHeaven (local storage) Records your cookie-consent choice and the date it was given, so the banner is not shown again until the choice expires. 180 days from the date your choice was saved

5. Consent Choices

When analytics or advertising tools are enabled, the website shows a cookie banner offering separate choices for analytics and for advertising. Refusing either category does not prevent access to the website. Your stored choice expires 180 days after you make it, after which the banner will appear again.

6. Manage or Withdraw Your Consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time. Use the button below to reset your stored choice — the cookie banner will then re-appear on the homepage so you can record a new choice.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website functionality, such as language preference storage or booking attribution.

7. Third Parties

Third-party analytics and advertising providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Providers currently planned or used are listed on the Sub-processors page.

8. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when our analytics, advertising, or consent setup changes. Material changes will trigger a fresh consent prompt on next visit.
